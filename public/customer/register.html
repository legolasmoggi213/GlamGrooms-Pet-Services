Governing laws

This website and its digital services are operated in compliance with the following Republic Acts of the Philippines:

  • Republic Act No. 10173 — Data Privacy Act of 2011: Requires the protection of personal data collected and processed through digital systems, including consent, security, and the rights of data subjects (access, correction, and erasure).
  • Republic Act No. 10175 — Cybercrime Prevention Act of 2012: Defines and penalizes offenses committed through digital systems (e.g., unauthorized access, data interference, and online fraud) and requires reasonable security measures to safeguard digital data.
  • Republic Act No. 10173 (Implementing Rules and Regulations): Establishes the National Privacy Commission as the regulatory body and mandates data protection officers, breach notification, and privacy impact assessments for automated processing of personal data.
